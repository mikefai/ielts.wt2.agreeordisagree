Examiner-approved method● ● ●
  1. Paraphrase + thesis — rewrite the question in your introduction, then state your position clearly in one sentence.
  2. Pick ONE side (or balanced, but lean). Examiners reward a clear, extended position over sitting on the fence.
  3. 2 body paragraphs — each = Topic sentence → Explain → Example → Link back.
  4. Concede once — one sentence acknowledging the other side boosts Task Response and Cohesion.
Safe template: Intro (paraphrase + thesis) → Body 1 (strongest reason) → Body 2 (second reason + concession) → Conclusion (restate)

How you’ll be marked (in 30 seconds)

Examiners score 4 criteria equally. Before you tick “done”, check: did you answer the opinion fully? Are ideas in logical paragraphs with linking? Is vocabulary precise with some less-common collocations? Are sentences mostly error-free with a mix of simple + complex?
